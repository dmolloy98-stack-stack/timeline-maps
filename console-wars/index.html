About the data

Nintendo and Sony hardware figures are company-reported. Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, PSP and PS Vita are estimates because the companies stopped reporting them. Game figures are reference points, not a ranking: some include PC copies, Microsoft reports players for recent games, and Wii Sports and Kinect Adventures! were bundled with hardware.

Sources include Nintendo's investor-relations sales data, Sony's quarterly results and Microsoft statements; the full list is in the data file.
