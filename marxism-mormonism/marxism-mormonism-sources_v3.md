# Joseph Smith vs. Karl Marx: source log (v3)

Map: `marxism-mormonism-map_v3` (36 × 24 in poster). Data as of Nov 16, 2026; fact-checked Nov 18, 2026.

Every item on the map was checked against at least two sources unless it is marked **single source** below. Every link here was opened and showed the cited content. `†` on the map means the item is disputed or rests on one source.

## Corrections made in v3 (after the fact-check)

| Item | v2 said | v3 says | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smith 1826 court case | "Tried, acquitted" | "Court hearing†, outcome disputed" | Sources disagree. Some say acquitted, some discharged, some guilty, and some call it a pre-trial examination (FAIR summarizes all the accounts). |
| City of Zion plat | "'equal material plane'" | "Jun 1833 · for up to 20,000 people" | The quoted phrase couldn't be found in any source. JSP gives the population figure. |
| J. Reuben Clark 1942 | "'individualistic, not communal'" | "'not communal nor communistic'" | That wording joined two separate sentences. The new text is a direct quote (Romney, Ensign 1977). |
| Benson 1961 | paraphrase | direct quote, shortened with "…" | Quinn quotes it as "No true Latter day Saint and no true American can be a socialist or a communist." |
| First Presidency Jan 1963 | "disavows Birch Society" | "rebukes Birch Society" | The statement "deplore[s] the presumption of … members of the John Birch Society." |
| Meeting Engels | "28 Aug 1844" | "Aug 1844" | The chronology gives 28 Aug; a second Marx/Engels Archive page gives "end of August." |
| Nauvoo | "1839–41" | "1839 · Commerce renamed Nauvoo" | Founding year; the 1841 date was temple cornerstones. |
| Bolshevik revolution | "first Marxist state" | "Bolsheviks take power" | History.com uses "first Marxist-Communist state" but EBSCO does not; the neutral wording is safer. |
| Plural-marriage revelation | † | no †; "recorded 12 Jul 1843 · published 1852" | D&C 132 heading and JSP agree. JSP: first published 14 Sep 1852. |
| Orderville, PRC 1949, communist-state list, Second Great Awakening | † | no † | Confirmed by two or more sources. |
| Era summary | Salt Lake 1847 under 1848–83; Edmunds Act 1882 under 1883–1917 | Moved/reworded | Dates fell outside their era columns. |
| McKay 1917 | no mark | † | Only one source (Prince) could be confirmed. |

## Single-source items (kept, marked †)

- **McKay 1917**, "It looks as if Russia will have a government 'by the people, of the people, and for the people'": Prince, Dialogue (2004). A second contemporary source was not found.
- **McKay 1951–52**, "we are facing Satan himself" (perspective box only): Prince, Dialogue (2004), quoting meeting minutes.
- **Membership "over 26,000" at Smith's death**: Church Newsroom.
