About the data

Award counts were checked in two independent sources for each player (Wikipedia's achievements lists and Basketball-Reference). Each date on the map was checked against at least two opened pages, except LeBron's 2003 draft position, which rests on a single source. The source log lists every link and notes where outlets disagree on a day.

All-Star counts run through 2025–26. LeBron's 2026–27 season is still being played, so no in-season numbers are printed. Season labels use the start year, so 1984 means the 1984–85 season.

A note on "Year 14"

By the end of Year 14, Jordan had 6 titles, Kobe 5 and LeBron 3. Jordan's Year 14 includes his baseball year, so he had played 13 seasons by then. At 13 seasons played the count is Jordan 6, Kobe 4, LeBron 3, so the ranking is the same either way.
