About the data

Every date, quote and number on the poster was checked against at least two sources. Items marked † are disputed (such as the outcome of Smith's 1826 court hearing) or rest on a single source (such as David O. McKay's 1917 remark on Russia). Ages are computed from birth dates.

LDS sources include the Joseph Smith Papers, Church History Topics, the Church Newsroom and BYU Studies. Marx sources include the Marx/Engels Internet Archive, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy and History.com. The source log lists every link along with the corrections made during fact-checking.

A labelled perspective

Some Latter-day Saints read the near-simultaneous rise of the Church (1830) and Marxism (1848) as spiritual opposition. In 1951–52 President David O. McKay told Church leaders "we are facing Satan himself" when speaking of Communists. Historians point instead to the context both movements shared: religious revivals, industrialization, the economic crises of 1837 and 1848, and the many communal experiments of the era. This poster presents both views and draws no conclusion.
